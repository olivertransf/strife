# Game of Strife

This document is the rules. During a game, play what it says. Change it between games, after the last player has finished.

It is the current Game of Life board, the 1–10 spinner, and the jobs, houses, and action cards listed here, with the house rules in this file. Amounts are thousands of dollars. 200K means 200,000 dollars.

The spinner shows 1 through 10. A result of 1–5 is red. A result of 6–10 is black. A six-sided die is used only for the child duel.

Two, three, or four play.

## Setup

Each player starts with 200K, one car, no insurance, no job, no spouse, no children, no houses, and no loans.

Everyone spins once. The highest spin takes the first seat. A tie spins again, and only the tied players respin. The others keep their relative order. After that ranking, the table may play one game of rock-paper-scissors. The winner of that game may move to the last seat. The other seats stay in the same order.

Each player then chooses a path:

- **Career.** Draw two jobs from the career deck. Keep one. Return the other to the bottom of that deck.
- **College.** Pay 100K to the bank. You do not draw a job yet. You will get one at Night School.

A player who cannot pay the 100K tuition in cash does not take loans for it. That player takes the career path instead.

In turn order, each player may invest in one star. The smiley star and the black-dot star are not open for this bet. Pay 100K in cash to the bank and place your marker on the chosen star. A star may hold more than one marker. A player who cannot pay 100K in cash skips the bet.

The first player then begins.

## A turn

1. If you are waiting in purgatory, resolve that death now. You are on the board again, on the space the death names, and the rest of this turn is a normal turn from there.
2. If you are quarantined, robbed into a skipped turn, or otherwise skipping, lose this turn. You do not spin and you do not move. Reduce the skip by one.
3. If you are jailed, spin only to test the key. Job bonuses from that spin still happen. A matching spin frees you. You do not move on the freeing spin. Any other spin leaves you jailed, after its bonuses.
4. If you are in a Groundhog loop, spin. Job bonuses happen. A black result ends the loop and you stay put this turn. A red result leaves you in the loop.
5. Otherwise spin, unless a card is giving you a fixed move. A fixed move of 10 spaces does not spin.
6. Turn the raw spin into a move length.
7. Step one space at a time. Passed spaces and the final space can each fire. A Stop, a Roadkill, a Car Stack, or a giraffe Payday ends the move early and throws away leftover steps.
8. Resolve the space you landed on. If it is a Stop you reached by moving forward, resolve it, then spin again and move again. If a card or the college rule placed you on a Stop, stay there. You do not spin again this turn.

Before the spin in step 5, you may repay one loan for 60K, buy one car for 500K if you are under your car cap, or buy insurance for 200K if you have none. Each of those takes cash. Loans cannot fund them. You may do any number of them, in any order, while you can pay.

## Movement

Start from the raw spin. Apply one multiplier, then the additives, then round down, then stop at 0. A length of 0 means you stay where you are and you do not trigger the space you are already on.

Multipliers:

- The Race Car Driver job doubles the raw spin.
- A private jet doubles the raw spin for two of your turns. If you already have the Race Car Driver double, the jet does nothing.
- Having no car halves the raw spin and cancels a double, including the jet.

Additives, all of them, after the multiplier:

- Aaron: +1 for each child Aaron has.
- Each boy: −1. The four boys from the twelve-child card are the exception covered on that card.
- E-bike: +2.
- EV: +1.
- Flat tire: −2 for three of your turns.
- Twelve-child card: −4 while any of those twelve children remain.

A fixed “move 10” from the race-car card ignores the multiplier, the additives, and the Race Car Driver death check. It lasts three of your turns, then you spin again.

### Passing and landing

You collect a Payday you pass. You collect a Payday you land on once, as a landing, not a second time as a pass. Moving backward does not collect Paydays.

These see every space you enter, passed or landed, in this order. The first one that removes you ends the list.

1. Board Roadkill, its expansion square, and any roadkill trail.
2. Car Stack.
3. Child duel, if both players have 5 or more children.
4. Police jail, if the mover is a Police Officer and the other player has a car.
5. Janitor, if the mover is a Janitor and the other player has a spouse.
6. Baby sitter, if the mover is a Baby Sitter and the other player has children.
7. Hit and run, if the mover has a car and the other player has none.

Ram is only checked when you land on it.

A giraffe car treats every Payday as the end of the move. You land on the first Payday you would enter, leftover steps are lost, and you do not spin again.

## The board

The path is the Game of Life track: a start split, later forks, Paydays, action spaces, house spaces, baby spaces, Stops, stars, a wheel space, and retirement. Direction of play is the printed direction. Pegs that share a space share that space.

Tag these spaces on the physical board. The rules below attach to the tags.

| Tag | Where it is | What landing or entering does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| School | The school space on the college path | Move to the Night School stop. Paydays, weddings, and other spaces on the stretch you skipped do not pay. |
| Night School | The Night School stop | Pay 100K unless you are the Janitor, then draw two jobs and keep one. |
| Roadkill | The third space after the landmark Stop, along play | Die forever. |
| Car Stack | The deadly space after Roadkill | Soft return. |
| Ram | The deadly space after Car Stack | Soft return, or a Zoo Keeper win if they land here. |
| Black-dot star | The star marked with a black dot | Bank pays you 1,000K. It is not an investment star. |
| Smiley star | The star printed with a smile | A normal star. It cannot take the setup kill-bet. |
| Macaw | The parrot space on the island stretch | Nigel sends you here. |
| Polar bear | The polar bear space | The retreat card starts here. |
| Risky fork | The fork onto the risky road | Spin when you choose it. |
| Family stop | The stop on the family side used for the early ending | See the early ending. |
| Wheel | The spin-to-win space | Spin the wheel. |

Other space kinds:

- **Payday.** Bank pays your payday income.
- **Action.** Draw one action card and resolve it. Broken leg skips the draw.
- **House.** You may buy the offered house for its printed price, in cash.
- **Baby.** A boy space adds one boy. A girl space adds one girl. A twins space adds one boy and one girl. A player with no car, or an infertile player, ignores the space.
- **Stop.** End the move, resolve the printed choice, then spin again when you reached it by moving forward.
- **Wedding Stop.** If you have a car and no spouse, gain a spouse. If the space prints a price, pay it. A player with no car ignores the wedding.
- **Star.** A normal star asks for an investment or, with the toll card, a 600K payment. See Stars.
- **Retirement.** You retire. See the end of the game.

The risky fork: when you choose the risky road, spin. Red sets your cash to 0. Your loans stay. Black has no extra effect. Then you take that road.

Going back onto a Stop by a card, by the school yeet, or by a backward move: stay. Do not take the extra spin.

## Pay, loans, payday

Payday income is your salary, after double salary or a salary flip. Then:

- The Pirate adds 150K.
- Each Eco House you own adds 10K.
- The Happy Loan Collector also takes 4 loans.

A player who has never had a job collects 0 on Payday. A player whose job was taken, who was fired into unemployment, or who came back from a death that stripped the job, collects 20K instead of a salary. That 20K is unemployment. The Pirate extra, the Eco House extra, and the loan collector’s loans do not apply to unemployment. The Bee Keeper has a job with salary 0, so the payday is 0 plus any Eco House, and Bee Keeper is not unemployment.

When a payment is required and your cash is short, take loans until you can pay. One loan gives 50K cash and one loan marker. Then pay.

Loans are forbidden for three things. If you cannot pay them in cash, the failure rule for that thing applies.

- Tuition. Failure: take the career path.
- A house, including the two houses on Love It or List It. Failure: do not buy.
- A star cost. Failure: Death Return.

On your turn, before you spin, you may pay 60K to remove one loan.

At the final tally each remaining loan subtracts 50K.

If a card steals all of a player’s money, the taker receives the cash and the loan markers. The victim’s cash and loan count become 0. A card that only sets cash to 0 leaves the loans where they are.

## Cars and insurance

Your cap is one car. The Bus Driver’s cap is two. A car is a car, an EV, or a giraffe. An e-bike is not a car.

Buy a car on your turn before you spin for 500K cash, if you are under your cap.

Insurance costs 200K cash and you can hold one policy. The next time you would lose a car, have a car stolen, or pay the flat-tire crash bill, discard the policy and cancel that one loss or that one bill. Insurance does not stop a hit-and-run bill.

Losing your last car removes your spouse and your children. They do not return when you gain a car later. The Bus Driver who still has one car keeps spouse and children, and moves at full speed.

With no car:

- Your multiplier is one half, and any double is off.
- When a player who has a car enters your space, you pay 200K. That is a hit and run. Insurance does not stop it.

An e-bike gives +2 and is not a car, so the half-speed rule and hit and run both apply.

An EV is a car and gives +1.

A giraffe is a car. You stop on every Payday.

## Family

Spouse and child cards do nothing when you do not have the person they act on. The divorce-fight card is the exception: it always jails you.

Children at the final tally are worth 100K each. Aaron also receives 100K from the bank at the moment each child arrives, and +1 movement for each child he currently has.

Each boy is −1 movement. Girls are not. Boys from the twelve-child card are exempt while they remain, because that card’s −4 replaces their penalties. When children are removed, remove those card boys first, then those card girls, then any other children.

When your move enters a space with another player and both of you have 5 or more children, you duel. Each of you rolls one die. The higher roll wins. A tie is rolled again. The loser loses 3 children. A loser with fewer than 3 loses all of them. The loser can never gain children again. Duel each such player in the order you reach them.

At the final tally you may sell your spouse. Spin once. Black pays you 200K. Red fines you 500K. Skipping the sale leaves the spouse unsold and unpaid.

A Janitor who enters the space of a player with a spouse removes that spouse forever. The player stays on the board.

## Jobs

The bonus number is the raw spin, before multipliers. When someone spins that number, each listed gift is paid by the spinner to the job holder. If the spinner is short, the spinner takes loans. Several holders of the same number are all paid.

Some jobs replace the 20K gift. Their special happens after every 20K gift from that spin has been paid.

Every result of 1–10 uses the order below. That includes the spin that starts a move, a jail spin, a Groundhog spin, the wheel, the Teepee spin, the YouTuber viral spin, and any card that tells a player to spin. On the food truck and on spin-to-lose, players spin in seat order. Each result finishes this order before the next player spins. Then compare the raw numbers that were actually spun.

1. Pay every 20K gift for this number.
2. Carpenter, on a 5: the bank pays the Carpenter 200K.
3. Draymond Green, on a 3: the spinner pays Draymond 200K.
4. Thief, on a 4: the Thief takes 200K from the spinner.
5. Assassin, on a 2: the spinner takes an Assassin hit.
6. Bee Keeper, on a 3, if anyone has that job: the spinner takes a Snake Return, and the bank pays the Bee Keeper 1,000K before the wipe.
7. If the spinner is the Race Car Driver and the raw spin is 9 or 10: Death Return.
8. Each Lifeguard who is not already dying from this spin takes the same death the victim took.
9. Only the spin that starts a move can produce movement. If that spinner is in purgatory or out, they do not move. Any other spin leaves positions alone. A spinner sent to purgatory by this spin does not receive the card’s own payout. The wheel still pays its prize, because the number was spun.

An Assassin hit on a Singer: the first hit silences the Singer and does not kill. The second hit restores the voice. Later hits do nothing. Silence has no engine effect. A life swap copies the silence and the hit count (0, 1, or done).

Anyone else hit by the Assassin takes a Death Return.

## Death and purgatory

A death other than Roadkill puts you in purgatory until your next turn. Until that turn starts, actions, job abilities, theft, lawsuits, police, the baby sitter, hit and run, and identity theft all miss you. You are off the path. At the start of your next turn the return happens, then you play from the space it names.

Roadkill removes you at once. You do not sit in purgatory. Your score is 0. You take no more turns.

| Death | What you keep | Where you go |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Roadkill | Nothing. You are out | Off the board |
| Soft return (Car Stack, Ram) | Cash, loans, job, houses, kept cards, and other statuses. Cars are given up. Losing the last car also removes spouse and children | Start |
| Death Return | One car. Cash is 0. Loans, job, family, houses, kept cards, and statuses are cleared. Star markers you bought stay on their stars | Start |
| Snake Return | One car and 5 loans. The 5 loans are taken after the wipe, so you also have the 250K those loans paid. Everything else on you is cleared | Start |
| Snakebite | Cash, loans, houses, cars, family, and other statuses. Your job becomes Bee Keeper and locks | Start |

Insurance may cancel the car loss on a soft return. One policy saves one car.

Ram, when the player landing there is the Zoo Keeper: that player wins immediately. They do not take the soft return.

Roadkill sources:

- The Roadkill space.
- If Roadkill Expands has been played, the space immediately before the Roadkill space as well.
- A Janitor removing a spouse. The spouse is gone. The player is not Roadkilled.
- The five spaces behind a player who has the roadkill blessing. “Behind” means the five spaces that player most recently left. Another player who enters one is Roadkilled. The owner of the trail is safe from their own trail.

A Lifeguard takes the same death as the player who just died, if the Lifeguard is a different player and is not already dying from this same event. The Lifeguard does not become Bee Keeper when the victim took Snakebite. In that one case the Lifeguard takes a Death Return. The 1,000K for a Snake Return is paid only to the player who held Bee Keeper when the 3 was spun. There is no second chain: deaths caused by the Lifeguard rule do not send another Lifeguard after the first mirror.

Snakebite and the Bee Keeper seat: the victim becomes Bee Keeper. If another player held Bee Keeper, that player loses the job, is fired forever, and cannot take a job for the rest of the game. Their payday is unemployment, 20K. A later Death Return clears fired-forever, because Death Return clears statuses.

The turtle penalty and the −1 spin are not used.

While you are in purgatory, a second death misses you. The first death stands.

## Jail, skips, and other statuses

**Police jail.** The officer names a key from 1 to 10. You spin on your turns. You leave jail when you spin the key, and you do not move that turn. Firefighters ignore the jail. A player who is already jailed stays on the existing key. Divorce jail uses key 4, and nobody chooses it.

**COVID.** Spin. Red: skip 3 turns. Black: skip 1 turn. This is the only COVID duration.

**Groundhog.** You spin each turn and stay until you spin black. The black spin does not move you.

**Broken leg.** For 5 of your turns, you draw no action cards. The counter drops at the end of each of your turns.

**Draw two.** Whenever you would draw one action, draw two and resolve them in the order drawn. Broken leg blocks this. Hogwarts uses its own draw and does not draw two extra on top.

**Odd half.** On an odd raw spin, your multiplier is one half. On an even raw spin, your other multiplier applies. This does not stack with the no-car half. A double is off on the odd spins.

**Flat tire.** −2 for three of your turns. If the length is below 0, it becomes 0, you do not move, and you pay 250K. Insurance can cancel that 250K.

**Jet.** Double for two of your turns, if you do not already have a double and you have a car.

**Move 10.** Three of your turns, move 10, no spin.

**Salary double.** Your printed salary is doubled. The Pirate’s 150K, each Eco House’s 10K, and unemployment sit outside that salary.

**Salary flip.** Spin. Red: your salary is 0. Black: your salary is 1,000K. This replaces the printed salary and clears a salary double. A double played after the flip doubles the flipped salary. 0 doubled is 0.

**Fired forever.** No job, unemployment payday, and Night School cannot give you a job. Snakebite can still make you Bee Keeper.

**Job lock (Bee Keeper).** Night School, steals, and the printed firing cards cannot remove this job.

**Left hand.** For the rest of the game you spin with your left hand at the table. The engine has no further effect.

**Star immunity.** You ignore star costs and star kill-bets. Other players’ cards and job abilities that target you do nothing. Your own cards still resolve. Board spaces that are not another player still resolve. Roadkill still applies.

**Bacteria.** You share the space of the player on your left and you move only when they move, copying their steps. You do not spin on those turns. Your cards, job, and money stay yours. If they skip or are jailed, you stay with them.

**Silence.** Table only, except that identity theft copies it.

## Stars

A star cost cannot be paid with a new loan. If you cannot pay a required star cost in cash, you take a Death Return.

The black-dot star pays 1,000K from the bank when you land on it. It does not ask for an investment and it does not take the setup bet.

The Carpenter ignores the first star cost that would hit them. Later star costs are paid in full.

**Setup bets.** If a player’s death is resolved while they are standing on a star that holds investment markers, those investors take the victim’s cash and loans before the death wipe. Split the cash as evenly as possible in whole K. Leftover K is wiped with the victim. Split the loan markers as evenly as possible. Any extra marker goes to the investor earliest in turn order. Star immunity on the victim cancels this collection. The black-dot star has no markers.

**Free investment.** The free-investment card places your marker on a star that has none. You pay nothing. The smiley star is allowed for this card. The black-dot star is not. The marker pays out like a setup bet.

**Life toll.** After you have played the 600K card, every later star you land on costs 600K, paid to the bank, or you take a Death Return. The black-dot star still pays you 1,000K and does not charge the toll. The Carpenter’s one ignore works on a toll.

## The wheel

The pot starts at 100K.

When the wheel is spun, each player picks a number from 1 to 10. Players may share a number. The player who landed spins. That spin uses the job order above. Each player who picked the result receives the current prize, including when the spinner was sent to purgatory. Then the pot grows by 100K.

The prize is the pot, except Lucky Moose, where the prize is 1,000K. The pot still grows by 100K after a Moose spin.

A retired player still picks a number.

## End of the game

You retire when you land on retirement. The bank pays the arrival bonus for the order of arrival: 400K, then 300K, then 200K, then 100K. A fifth arrival would pay 0. There are at most four players.

A retired player does not move, does not collect Paydays, and skips weddings. They still pick wheel numbers, and they can still be chosen for identity theft.

**Early stop.** If every other player is retired, and you are more than 50 spaces from retirement along your path, move to the family stop and stop. You do not take an arrival bonus. Then tally.

A player who was Roadkilled scores 0 and is not “retired” for the early-stop test. A player in purgatory is still in the game, so the early stop waits.

**Tally**, for each player still in the game:

1. Cash, including arrival bonuses already paid.
2. Spin each house. Add the red or black sale price.
3. Add 100K per child.
4. Add 100K per kept card.
5. The optional spouse sale.
6. Subtract 50K per loan.

The highest total wins. A tie is a shared win. The Zoo Keeper’s Ram win skips this tally.

## Action cards

Draw from the action deck. When it is empty, shuffle the discard into a new deck. Unless a card says it is kept, it goes to the discard after it resolves. Kept cards score 100K at the tally and sit with that player until stolen by a full life swap.

The Pirate’s reversal applies to any amount this catalog would make the Pirate pay the bank. The bank pays the Pirate instead. Money paid to another player is unchanged.

### Money

**Mint crypto.** Spin. Black: bank pays you 1,000K. Red: pay 100K.

**Crypto scams.** Spin. Red: pay 500K. Black: bank pays you 500K. You do not go to police jail.

**ChatGPT.** Spin. Black: lose your job and take unemployment. Red: bank pays you 500K.

**Lawsuit, tomatoes.** Choose another player. They pay you 100K. Keep this card.

**Lawsuit, stolen idea.** Choose another player. They pay you 100K. Keep this card.

**Lawsuit, damaged property.** Choose another player. They pay you 100K. Keep this card.

**Lucky Moose.** Spin the wheel. The prize for this spin is 1,000K. Then the pot grows by 100K.

**Goat ribbon.** Bank pays you 120K. Keep this card.

**Food truck.** Every player spins. Each player who tied for the highest result receives 500K from the bank.

**Spin to lose.** Every player spins. Each player who tied for the highest result pays 500K.

**Dog ransom.** Spin. 1–3 pay 10K. 4–6 pay 20K. 7–9 pay 50K. 10 pay 100K.

**Movie star.** Spin. 1–5 pay 50K. 6–8 the bank pays you 50K. 9–10 the bank pays you 5,000K.

**Mugged.** Spin. Black: no loss. Red: your cash becomes 0. Loans stay.

**Robbed by the player on your right.** That player takes all your cash and all your loans. You skip your next turn.

**Steal from the left.** You take all cash and all loans from the player on your left.

**Identity theft.** Choose another player who is still in the game. Swap your whole record with theirs: cash, loans, job, family, cars, houses, kept cards, space, retired flag, and every status, including purgatory, jail, silence, and the Assassin hit count. Seats stay put, so the turn order does not change. Star immunity on the chosen player makes the card fail.

**Five-loan hobby.** Take 5 loans.

**Double salary.** Double your printed salary for the rest of the game, under the salary-flip rule above.

**Salary flip.** Spin. Red: salary 0. Black: salary 1,000K. Replaces the printed salary and clears an older double.

### Homes

**House collector.** Spin. 1–5 steal nothing. 6–8 steal one house. 9–10 steal two houses. You choose which houses and from whom. Stolen houses join your row. The victims are not paid.

**Love it or list it.** Take two house cards from the house deck. You may buy each for 200K cash. You may buy one, both, or neither. Unbought cards return to the bottom. Loans cannot pay the 200K.

**Steal a job or a house.** Choose one: take one other player’s job, or take one house from them. A stolen job puts you in that job and puts your old job in its discard. The victim is unemployed. A stolen house moves to your row. A locked job cannot be taken and cannot be given away. If you are fired forever, the job steal fails. Star immunity on the victim makes the steal fail.

**Foreclosure.** If you own any house, return all of your houses to the house discard. If you own none, remove all of your children.

**Free star.** Place your investment marker on a star that has no marker. The black-dot star is excluded. You pay 0.

**Life toll.** For the rest of the game, landing on a star other than the black-dot star costs 600K or Death Return.

**Nigel.** Move to the Macaw space. Take 3 loans. This placement is a drop, not a forward move: you do not collect spaces you skipped. Resolve Macaw if it has a printed effect, then your turn ends. You do not spin again.

### Cars

**Win a car.** If you are under your cap, gain a car. If you are at your cap, the bank pays you 60K.

**Car flip.** If you have a car, lose one. Insurance can cancel that loss. If you have no car, gain a car.

**Insurance flip.** If you have insurance, discard it. If you have none, gain a policy for free.

**Unpaid insurance.** Lose one car. Insurance can cancel that loss. For the rest of the game, an odd raw spin uses the odd-half multiplier.

**Flat tire.** For three of your turns, −2 to the move. A result below 0 does not move you and bills you 250K.

**Giraffe.** Your car becomes a giraffe. If you have no car, the card does nothing.

**E-bike.** If insurance cancels the car loss, the card does nothing and you keep the car. Otherwise lose the car (and spouse and children, if it was your last) and gain the e-bike +2.

**EV.** If you have a car, it becomes an EV and you gain +1. If you have no car, gain an EV for free. An EV counts as a car.

**Steal every car.** Each other player loses one car. Insurance can cancel that player’s loss. For each car actually lost, if you are under your cap, gain one car. Cars past your cap are discarded. A player who loses their last car takes the no-car rules.

**Move 10.** For three of your turns, move 10 spaces and do not spin.

**Jet.** For two of your turns, double your raw spin, if you have a car and you do not already have a double.

### Work

**Fired, cat.** Discard your job. Draw two from that job’s deck and keep one. A locked Bee Keeper keeps Bee Keeper, and the drawn cards go to the bottom.

**Fired, snooze.** Same as Fired, cat.

**Fired forever.** Lose your job. You are unemployed and cannot be given a job. Snakebite can still make you Bee Keeper.

**YouTuber** is a job, not a card. The viral spin is in the job list.

### Family

**Adoption.** If you have a car and you are not infertile, gain one girl and move back 10 spaces. Backward movement does not collect Paydays. If you cannot gain the child, the card does nothing.

**Twelve children.** If you have a car and you are not infertile, gain 8 girls and 4 boys. Those four boys do not each apply −1. Instead you have −4 movement while any of the twelve remain. If all twelve are gone, the −4 ends. If you cannot gain children, the card does nothing.

**Kidnapping.** If you have no children, the card does nothing. Pay 10K per child. Then spin. Black: the children stay. Red: the children are removed.

**Vows.** If you have no spouse, the card does nothing. Spin. Black: remove the spouse. Red: pay 100K and keep the spouse.

**Divorce fight.** Remove your spouse and your children if you have them. You go to divorce jail, key 4. A Firefighter removes the family and does not go to jail. This card resolves even when you have no family.

### Movement and body

**Lost phone.** Move back 6. Do not collect Paydays on the way. Resolve the space you stop on. If it is a Stop, do not spin again.

**Night school.** Move to the Night School stop, pay the fee (0 for the Janitor), and draw as Night School. Do not spin again.

**Polar bear.** Move to the polar bear space, then move backward until you are on the space immediately before a star. Do not land on that star. Do not collect Paydays while retreating. Resolve the space you stop on. If it is a Stop, do not spin again.

**COVID.** Spin. Red: skip 3 turns. Black: skip 1 turn.

**Broken leg.** Draw no actions for the next 5 of your turns.

**Left hand.** Spin with your left hand for the rest of the game.

**Groundhog.** Stay until you spin black, as in the status list.

**Meat.** If you have eaten meat in the last 24 hours, your cash becomes 0. Loans stay. If you have not, the card does nothing. In software, ask that player.

**Hogwarts.** Draw two actions. Keep one and resolve it. Discard the other unrevealed to the bottom. This draw ignores the draw-two status.

**Draw two.** For the rest of the game, an action draw is two cards, resolved in order.

**Wheel card.** Resolve a wheel spin on the current pot.

**Bacteria.** Attach to the player on your left, as in the status list.

**Break free.** If you are bacteria, or you are the host of a bacteria, detach. The bacteria player moves one space forward from the host and draws one action. If neither of you is in that pair, the card does nothing.

**Star immunity.** For the rest of the game, ignore star costs, ignore star kill-bets, and ignore other players’ targeted cards and job abilities.

**Roadkill blessing.** The five spaces behind you are Roadkill for everyone else, for the rest of the game.

**Roadkill expands.** The space immediately before the board’s Roadkill space is also Roadkill.

**Snakebite.** Resolve the Snakebite death. You become Bee Keeper. Any previous Bee Keeper is fired forever.

**Jet, move 10, flat tire, giraffe, EV, e-bike.** Covered under Cars and under statuses.

## Worked examples

**Assassin and Singer.** The raw spin is 2. The Firefighter holds bonus 2, so the spinner pays the Firefighter 20K. The Assassin special then hits. The Singer has no hits yet, so the Singer is silenced and stays alive. The spinner moves at the modified length of 2. Later, another player spins 2. Gifts are paid again. This second hit restores the Singer’s voice. The Singer still does not die.

**Bee Keeper and a 3.** A Zoo Keeper and a Race Car Driver are in the game, so both receive 20K from the spinner. Draymond then collects 200K from the spinner. The Bee Keeper is paid 1,000K by the bank. The spinner then Snake Returns: on their next turn they appear at start with one car, 5 loans, and 250K from those loans. They do not move on the turn they spun the 3. If the spinner is the Bee Keeper, the 1,000K is paid and the Snake Return wipe removes it.

**Snakebite while someone is already Bee Keeper.** The victim goes to start, keeps money and belongings, and becomes Bee Keeper. The old Bee Keeper is fired forever. A Lifeguard in the game takes a Death Return, not a second Bee Keeper seat.

**No car.** An e-bike trade drops your last car, your spouse, and your children. A raw spin of 7 becomes 3 (half of 7, rounded down) and then +2 from the bike, so you move 5. A player with a car who enters your space bills you 200K.

**College.** You paid 100K at setup. Your move reaches the school space. You are placed on Night School. Paydays you would have passed on that skipped stretch pay nothing. You pay 100K and draw two college jobs. The Janitor fee waiver does not apply, because you are not the Janitor yet. You do not spin again this turn.

## Developer model

Software should implement this file and should not invent a missing amount, a missing target, or an extra victory rule. If a card’s target is illegal, the card fails and is discarded.

Bee Keeper’s `job_locked` is true. Carpenter’s `star_ignore` starts at 1. Everyone else starts at 0.

Car cap is 2 when `job` is Bus Driver, otherwise 1. `has_car` is true when `car` is not `"none"`.

Children = `girls + boys`.

### Payday formula

If `job` is null and `unemployed` is false: 0.

If `unemployed` is true and `job` is null: 20.

Otherwise: salary after override and double, plus 150 if the job is Pirate, plus 10 per Eco House. Then, if the job is Happy Loan Collector, take 4 loans.

### Move length

```
raw = spin          # 1..10
mult = 1
if job is race_car_driver: mult = 2
if jet_turns > 0 and mult == 1 and has_car: mult = 2
if not has_car: mult = 0.5
if odd_half and raw is odd: mult = 0.5

length = raw * mult
length += 1 per child, if job is aaron
length -= (boys - crowd_boys)
length += 2 if ebike
length += 1 if car is ev
length -= 2 if flat_tire_turns > 0
length -= 4 if crowd_girls + crowd_boys > 0
length = floor(length)
if length < 0: length = 0
if flat_tire_turns > 0 and (raw * mult + additives) < 0:
    charge 250 unless insurance cancels it
```

`move10_turns > 0` replaces this whole formula with 10, and does not call the Race Car Driver death check.

### Turn pipeline

1. `resolve_purgatory`
2. `skip_or_jail_or_groundhog`
3. `optional_purchases` (repay 60, car 500, insurance 200)
4. `spin_or_fixed`
5. `job_spin_triggers` (even on a jail spin or a groundhog spin)
6. `modify_length`
7. `walk`
8. `land`
9. `end_of_turn` (decrement broken leg, flat tire, jet, move 10)

`job_spin_triggers` runs before the walk, and a death from that step cancels the walk.

Walk, for each entered space, stop on the first that removes the mover:

1. `roadkill`
2. `car_stack` → queue soft return, stop the walk
3. `child_duel`
4. `police_jail`
5. `janitor_spouse`
6. `babysitter`
7. `hit_and_run`
8. If this space is not the final space and it is a Payday, pay payday
9. If this space is a Stop, the school space, or a Payday and the mover’s car is a giraffe, make it the final space

Land:

1. Ram and the mover is Zoo Keeper → that player wins, game over
2. Ram → queue soft return
3. Payday → pay payday once
4. Otherwise the space tag (action, house, baby, wedding, star, wheel, school, retirement, night school)
5. If the space is the school space, move to Night School, resolve Night School, and do not spin again
6. If the space is a Stop reached by forward movement, go back to step 4 of the pipeline (spin again). A drop, a backward landing, or the school yeet does not spin again

### Deaths

```
roadkill:
    out = true
    score = 0

soft_return:
    if insurance: insurance = false, keep one car
    else: car = none, and if that was the last car: spouse = false, girls = 0, boys = 0, crowd_girls = 0, crowd_boys = 0
    space = start
    # queued through purgatory unless this is already resolve_purgatory

death_return:
    cash = 0
    loans = 0
    job = null
    unemployed = true
    fired_forever = false
    job_locked = false
    clear family, crowd_girls, crowd_boys, houses, kept_cards, insurance, statuses, ebike
    car = "car"
    space = start
    # investment markers on stars stay

snake_return:
    same clear as death_return
    car = "car"
    take 5 loans    # cash becomes 250, loans becomes 5
    # the Bee Keeper's 1000 is paid before this clear, to the holder at spin time

snakebite:
    previous Bee Keeper, if another player: job = null, fired_forever = true, unemployed = true
    job = bee_keeper, job_locked = true, job_deck = career
    space = start
    # belongings stay
```

Death Return sets cash to 0 and clears loans, so those loans are not subtracted at the tally.

Purgatory stores the death id. `resolve_purgatory` applies the table, then clears purgatory, then the turn continues from the new space. Roadkill does not enter purgatory.

### Payments

`pay(amount)` takes loans in steps of 50 until `cash >= amount`, then subtracts `amount`. It refuses loans when the reason is `tuition`, `house`, or `star`. A refused star payment calls Death Return. A refused house payment cancels the purchase. A refused tuition selects the career path.

`steal_all(from, to)` moves `cash` and `loans` from `from` to `to`.

`grant(amount)` adds `amount` to cash from the bank.

### Win check

After any step:

- Zoo Keeper landed on Ram → that player wins.
- A player retired, or the early-stop rule moved the last mover to the family stop, and no non-retired, non-out player remains who still has to travel → tally.

Early stop: every other player has `retired` true, none of those others are merely `out`, the active player’s remaining spaces to retirement are greater than 50, and nobody is in purgatory. Move the active player to the family stop, set them retired without an arrival bonus, and tally.
